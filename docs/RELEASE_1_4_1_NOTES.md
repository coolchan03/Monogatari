# Monogatari 1.4.1

The Library now covers every kind of media, and Discover opens on a mix of everything.

## New

- **Library tab for all your media.** The old *On device* tab only listed downloaded manga. *Library* shows everything together: your history in a *Continue* strip, favourites, and one *On this device* section for downloaded manga and comics, imported books, and your own video and audio files. A chip row (All · Manga · Books · Video · Audio) filters it, and *On this device* narrows it to what is stored here.
- **Recommended, for every medium.** A strip at the bottom of the Library follows the chip you pick: manga ranked by what you read, light novels and free books, trending anime and video, audiobooks, or a mix for All. It loads after your own files appear and also shows on a new, empty library. Tap to open, long-press to add to your library.
- **Discover has an All option**, now the first chip and the default: a mix of trending manga, books, anime and audio plus picks from your reading. *Search everything* searches every kind of source. The other chips are unchanged.
- **New default navigation bar:** Library · Updates · History · Discover · More. One time only: a bar you never changed moves to this layout, and a customised bar just swaps *On device* for *Library*. You can still change tabs in Settings > Appearance & navigation.

## Known limits

- Recommendations come from AniList (with MyAnimeList as a backup) and a few free catalogs; if none can be reached the strip simply stays hidden.
- Passed unit tests, the release build and an Android 15 start-up test; device testing follows.

## Upgrading

Installs over any 1.0.x to 1.4.0 build (same package and signing key). No database changes.
