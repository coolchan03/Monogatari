# Monogatari 1.0.1

Fixes and polish from the first round of owner feedback on 1.0.0.

## Changes

- **Light or dark follows your phone.** The app no longer starts in dark mode until a theme is chosen.
- **Settings is one tap away:** a gear icon on the main screen's top bar.
- **One look across screens.** Books, Library, podcast and video feeds, News, Discover, the EPUB and PDF readers, the episode player, Nearby transfer, WebDAV, backup import, Source log, StoryWeaver and the FanFiction/FictionPress browser now share the same style: one main button, chips for choices, and secondary actions in the ⋮ menu.
- **Feeds come pre-installed.** Podcasts: Escape Pod, Welcome to Night Vale, Pop Culture Happy Hour, PodCastle, Pseudopod. News: Anime News Network, Anime Expo, Standard Ebooks. News loads straight away. Feeds you remove stay removed.
- **New Kitsune artwork.** Every mascot image now comes from the full-resolution originals (512px instead of 64px enlarged), with new News reporter and Audio forms.
- **Sharper video thumbnails** for YouTube, Dailymotion and Twitch.

## Not in this release

Drafts of v1.1+ features (Mihon import, sync conflicts, side-by-side translation and others) stay on the development branch until they are finished and tested.
